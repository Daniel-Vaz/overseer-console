# Cloud storage

Optional. The console can link to your own cloud storage to carry saves, games and mods between devices. It has no
app of its own on any of them: every player creates one in their own account, with access to the console's files only,
and links the console to it. It links to one cloud at a time.

| Cloud | Free room | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dropbox | 2 GB: the two games and a mod | [Dropbox link](dropbox.md) |
| Google Drive | Up to 15 GB, shared with Gmail and Google Photos | [Google Drive link](google-drive.md) |

A game is about 550 MB; the mods the console knows are 0.4 to 3 GB each as they are downloaded, but for Fallout
Yesterday, whose unpacked folder is 9 GB. For both games with a few mods, Google Drive has the room.

What a linked cloud does is the same in either, and is described in [Features](features.md): saves
kept as one zip per slot, games and mods loaded from it, and **Put in your cloud**, which sends them from this device.
One thing only Dropbox does: when a mod the console knows is installed from a zip that needs no sign-in (see [Mods](mods.md)),
Dropbox downloads it into the Mods folder itself. Google Drive cannot download from the web, so there your browser downloads it, and
the console offers to put it in the Mods folder once it is loaded.
